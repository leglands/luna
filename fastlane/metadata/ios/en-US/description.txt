Luna is a privacy-first period tracker that keeps all your data encrypted on your device. Track your cycle, predict your next period, monitor symptoms, mood, and BBT. Zero network - your data never leaves your phone.

Features:
- Cycle prediction with confidence intervals
- Daily symptom, mood, and energy tracking
- Basal body temperature (BBT) charting
- Fertile window estimation
- Multiple tracking modes: Regular, TTC, Pregnant, Perimenopause
- Calm Mode to reduce anxiety
- Encrypted backup and restore
- Emergency data wipe (panic button)
- 47 languages, 4 themes (light/dark/contrast)
- VoiceOver and Dynamic Type support

Your privacy matters. Luna uses military-grade encryption (AES-256-GCM) and never connects to the internet. No account required. No data collection. Period.
