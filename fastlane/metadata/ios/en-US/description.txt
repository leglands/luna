Luna is a period tracker that keeps all your data encrypted on your device. Track your cycle, predict your next period, monitor symptoms, mood, and BBT. Zero network: your data never leaves your phone.

Features:
- Cycle prediction with confidence intervals
- Daily symptom, mood, and energy tracking
- Basal body temperature (BBT) charting
- Period reminders
- Fertile window and ovulation estimation
- Multiple tracking modes: Regular, TTC, Pregnant, Perimenopause
- Calm Mode to reduce anxiety
- Encrypted backup and restore
- Emergency data wipe (panic button)
- 4 themes (light, dark, contrast)
- VoiceOver and Dynamic Type support

Luna uses AES-256-GCM encryption and never connects to the internet. No account required. No data collection.
